# Web Tracking Privacy Research

Senior Research Project

## Project Overview

This research examines web-tracking technologies and privacy practices across different categories of websites.

The study focuses on stateful and stateless tracking methods, cookie security practices, and how U.S. privacy laws address evolving web-tracking technologies.

## Research Questions

1. What technologies are being used as alternatives to traditional third-party cookies?
2. To what extent do websites follow recommended cookie security practices?
3. How are major U.S. privacy laws addressing newer web-tracking technologies?

Research and development are currently in progress.
